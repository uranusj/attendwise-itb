### 📚 AttendWise — GNDEC Attendance Tracker

**AttendWise** is an Android application designed for GNDEC students to manage and track their lecture-wise attendance efficiently. The app integrates timetable information with attendance tracking to help students maintain the required **75% attendance** and make smarter decisions about attending upcoming lectures.

### ✨ Key Features

* 📊 Subject-wise and overall attendance tracking
* 🗓️ Daily and weekly timetable
* 👥 Support for **ITB, B1 & B2** sections
* 🏫 Classroom and lecture information
* 🔔 Lecture reminders and notifications
* 🧮 Smart calculation of lectures required to reach 75%
* 🎯 Calculates how many lectures can be missed while maintaining 75%
* 📈 Attendance history and analytics
* 🔄 Timetable synchronization/import
* 📝 Manual attendance marking
* 👨‍💻 Admin timetable management
* 🌐 Designed around GNDEC's timetable structure
* 📱 Modern, student-friendly Android UI

**Goal:** Make attendance management simple, accurate, and proactive—so students always know where they stand and which lectures matter most. 🚀

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin • Android • Jetpack Compose • Firebase • MVVM/Clean Architecture
